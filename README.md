# SlabIQ Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 27 March 2026**

SlabIQ is built for Australian concreters. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

---

## Who we are

SlabIQ is developed and operated by Jake Murphy (ABN registered), based in Queensland, Australia. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: **slabiqapp@gmail.com**

---

## What information we collect

### Information you enter directly

When using SlabIQ you may enter:

- **Project and estimate details** — slab dimensions, zone names, material specifications, costs
- **Client information** — client name, phone number, and email address
- **Business details** — your business name, ABN, and bank details (for invoice generation)

This information is stored **locally on your device only** using AsyncStorage. It is not transmitted to our servers and is not accessible to us.

### Floor plan images and PDFs

When you use the AI Floor Plan Reader feature, images or PDFs of floor plans are sent to our secure server for analysis by an AI model. These files are:

- Transmitted securely over HTTPS
- Processed to extract slab dimensions only
- **Not stored** on our servers after processing
- **Not used** to train AI models

### Subscription information

If you subscribe to SlabIQ Pro, your subscription is managed by RevenueCat (revenuecat.com). RevenueCat may collect:

- A device identifier
- Purchase and subscription history
- App version and platform information

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not receive or store your payment card details.

### Usage data

The app tracks locally, on your device only:

- Number of estimates created (to enforce the free tier limit)
- Whether you have completed onboarding

This data never leaves your device.

## How we use your information

| Information | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Estimate and project data | Display, calculate, and export your quotes |
| Client details | Pre-fill quotes, invoices, and email summaries |
| Business details | Generate tax invoices |
| Floor plan images | AI dimension extraction (processed then discarded) |
| Subscription status | Determine access level (free or Pro) |

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties except as described above (RevenueCat for subscription management, Anthropic's AI API for floor plan analysis).

---

## Data storage and security

All estimate data, client details, and business information are stored **on your device only**. We do not operate a database of user data.

Floor plan images are transmitted to our server using HTTPS encryption and are discarded immediately after processing.

Our server infrastructure is hosted on Vercel (vercel.com) in the United States. Floor plan images transit through this server but are not retained.

---

## Your rights

You can delete all data stored by SlabIQ at any time by uninstalling the app. Because we do not store your personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our end.

For subscription-related data held by RevenueCat, contact RevenueCat directly at revenuecat.com or through their support channels.
